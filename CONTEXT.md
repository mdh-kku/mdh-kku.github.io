# MDH Website

A lab website presenting the MDH Lab (one Cohort, their Advisors, and their work) to Partners.

## Lab & degree

**MDH**:
The Lab's short name, standing for Medical Data Science and Health Informatics.
_Avoid_: using MDH to mean the degree on the site

**Lab**:
The research group the website presents: one Cohort, shown with their Advisors. Full name "Medical Data Science and Health Informatics"; short name MDH.

**MSc**:
KKU Faculty of Medicine's Master of Science in Data Science and Health Informatics (วิทยาศาสตรมหาบัณฑิต สาขาวิทยาการข้อมูลและสารสนเทศสุขภาพ), taken by medical students as a dual degree alongside the MD. 10 seats a year.
_Avoid_: MDH (that's the Lab), dual-degree track

**Cohort**:
The students admitted to the MSc (with the MD) in the same year (รุ่น). The site currently covers one cohort of 9 students.
_Avoid_: generation, batch, class

## People & research

**Advisor**:
A faculty member who supervises a student's MSc thesis (อาจารย์ที่ปรึกษา).
_Avoid_: supervisor, mentor

**Research interest**:
A one- to two-sentence statement of what a person studies.
_Avoid_: bio, about me

**Thesis topic**:
A generalized public description of a student's MSc thesis; not its exact title.
_Avoid_: thesis title

**Project**:
A piece of work by lab members with a named team, such as SPEAK SPARK! or Kidney Guide. No single project is the site's central story.
_Avoid_: flagship, product, protagonist

**Update**:
A dated, sourced item shown under Latest: an award, a milestone, a talk, or a paper.
_Avoid_: news post, achievement (an award is one kind of Update)

**Contributor**:
A person outside the Cohort who is credited on a lab project, such as a non-MDH medical student.
_Avoid_: collaborator (that word means a Partner), member

## Audience

**Partner**:
A funder, collaborator, researcher, or other university the site must win over. The site's only audience.
_Avoid_: stakeholder, industry, applicant
